Agency: Hamilton County EMA (demo)
Title: Boil Water Advisory — Green Township
Body:
Residents of Green Township should boil tap water for at least 1 minute before drinking, cooking, or brushing teeth until further notice. A water main break on Harrison Avenue may have allowed contaminants into the system. Bottled water is available at the Green Township Senior Center, 3620 Epley Rd, 9 AM–7 PM.
